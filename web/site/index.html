Everything a console does. Everything a PC can.

Controller first

PS5 DualSense, PS4 DualShock and Xbox controllers work from the first boot, wired or Bluetooth. No keyboard or mouse needed, ever.

All your games

Steam, Epic, GOG and games you installed yourself appear together on Home, with their artwork, automatically.

Free games

Current giveaways and free-to-play games from the big stores, one press from Home.

Downloads built in

A web browser you drive with the controller and a torrent downloader. Installers and archives become games in your library.

Performance monitor

Live CPU, GPU, memory and temperatures, an in-game overlay, and End task for anything that hangs.

Make it yours

Nine themes, your own wallpaper, navigation sounds and startup music.

Install in four steps

  1. 1

    Get Windows

    Download the free Windows 11 ISO from microsoft.com ("Download Windows 11 Disk Image").

  2. 2

    Run the PC5 OS Installer

    Download and open PC5-OS-Setup.exe. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info → Run anyway (the app isn't code-signed yet).

  3. 3

    Choose where

    Next to Windows on its own partition (nothing is deleted), on a USB drive for another PC, or as an ISO. Pick how much to strip.

  4. 4

    Restart into PC5 OS

    The boot menu now lists PC5 OS. The first start sets Windows up, then the console opens with your controller ready.

Questions

Does it replace my Windows?

No. Installed next to Windows, PC5 OS is a separate, stripped-down Windows on its own partition. Each time the PC starts you choose PC5 OS or your usual Windows, and your files, apps and games stay where they are.

What does "stripped down" remove?

You choose: ads and suggestions, News, Copilot, Office and Teams installers, Phone Link, OneDrive, old extras like Internet Explorer mode, and more. Steam and 7-Zip are added. Everything removed can be reinstalled later, and PC5 OS still gets Windows updates.

Is my Windows licence OK?

PC5 OS is built from your own Windows ISO. On a PC that already runs Windows 10 or 11, the same edition normally activates by itself.

How do I get back to my usual Windows?

In PC5 OS: Settings → System → Restart into another OS. Or pick Windows in the boot menu when the PC starts. Inside PC5 OS, Desktop Mode also opens the full desktop with the controller as a mouse.

How do I remove PC5 OS?

From your usual Windows, open the PC5 OS Installer and choose Remove next to PC5 OS: the partition and boot entry are deleted and the space goes back to your drive.

Do my PlayStation controllers work in every game?

PC5 adds the games you install yourself to Steam, so Steam Input translates your PlayStation controller for games that only understand Xbox controllers.

Is it free?

Yes. Store games are bought or claimed through their own stores (Steam, Epic, GOG).
